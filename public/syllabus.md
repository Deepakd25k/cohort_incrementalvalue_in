# IncrementalValue D2C Performance Marketing — Cohort 03 Syllabus

## WEEK 1: Start with the business & the data
Unit economics · GTM · GA4 · Tracking QA

**SESSION 01: What can this brand afford to acquire?**
AOV, acquisition cost, contribution, discounts, shipping and RTO. Read ad results alongside the economics of delivered orders.
*Output:* A unit-economics sheet with clear assumptions.

**SESSION 02: Can you trust the events you’re reading?**
Map the buying journey. Inspect GTM/GA4 events, UTMs and Pixel/CAPI concepts. Practise checking whether an event fires correctly.
*Output:* A tracking map and event-validation checklist.

## WEEK 2: Find the customer. Design the test.
Creative strategy · Meta · Google · AI research

**SESSION 03: Turn customer objections into creative angles**
Research the customer, offer and competition. Use AI to draft and organise insights, then check them against real evidence.
*Output:* A customer brief and three testable creative angles.

**SESSION 04: Make campaign decisions you can explain**
Meta and Google channel roles, account structure, testing and budget decisions. Compare platform reports with store data; examine attribution overlap and its limits.
*Output:* A campaign testing plan and performance explanation.

## WEEK 3: Fix what happens after the click
CRO tactics · Checkout · Payments · COD & RTO

**SESSION 05: Diagnose the page before redesigning it**
Message match, offer clarity, product information, trust placement, mobile friction and checkout steps. Prioritise hypotheses and design tests with success measures.
*Output:* A CRO audit and a prioritised experiment backlog.

**SESSION 06: Follow the order beyond the purchase event**
Payment failures, prepaid/COD mix, verification, cancellations and delivery outcomes. Identify what marketing can fix and what needs operations support.
*Output:* An order-quality diagnosis and action checklist.

## WEEK 4: Connect the system. Show your thinking.
Retention · n8n · Brand discovery · Applications

**SESSION 07: Use AI and automation with a clear purpose**
Map a retention flow and the roles of marketplaces and quick commerce. Walk through a small n8n workflow: structured inputs → AI-assisted summary → human review.
*Output:* A retention map and a guided workflow outline.

**SESSION 08: Find relevant brands. Make a specific approach.**
Research brands through public ad libraries, LinkedIn, store sites and career pages. Assess fit, identify the right public contact and write an evidence-led application or pitch. Review selected final case plans.
*Output:* A brand shortlist, personalised approach and 30-day action plan.
